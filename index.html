What should I know after this lecture?

Core mechanics

Interpret amount and accumulation functions, move cash flows between dates with accumulation factors, distinguish simple from compound growth, and handle nominal rates, compounding frequency and continuous compounding.

Rate language

Convert between nominal and effective interest rates, interpret force of interest, and distinguish interest rates from discount rates by the denominator used.

Valuation

Compute future and present values of one or many cash flows, including deposits made after time 0 and discounted-loan cash flows.

Economic meaning

Understand inflation, purchasing power, and real interest: nominal money growth can be positive while purchasing power falls.

\(a(t)=A(t)/A(0)\), with \(a(0)=1\)
↓ compare endpoints

\(i_{[t_1,t_2]}=a(t_2)/a(t_1)-1\)
↔ change denominator
Effective discount rate
\(d_{[t_1,t_2]}=(a(t_2)-a(t_1))/a(t_2)\)
\(i=d/(1-d)\)
↓ infinitesimal growth
Force of interest
\(\delta_t=a'(t)/a(t)\)
↑ integrate
Recover accumulation

↓ invert
